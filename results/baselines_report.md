# Random-escalation baseline and bootstrap confidence intervals

_Generated by scoring/baselines_bootstrap.py. Seed 42. 450 labeled questions, 98 wrong (base error rate 0.218)._

## Part 1 — Gate vs random escalation

Random baseline: the same number of questions per category as the final gate, picked at random, 10,000 times.

| Category | Escalated | Gate precision | Random precision (mean, 95% range) | p (random ≥ gate) | Gate recall | Random recall (mean, 95% range) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| factual | 23 | 0.957 (22/23) | 0.332 (0.174–0.522) | 0.0000 | 0.440 | 0.153 (0.080–0.240) |
| math | 9 | 0.222 (2/9) | 0.040 (0.000–0.222) | 0.0419 | 0.333 | 0.060 (0.000–0.333) |
| reasoning | 23 | 0.435 (10/23) | 0.280 (0.130–0.435) | 0.0640 | 0.238 | 0.153 (0.071–0.238) |
| all | 55 | 0.618 (34/55) | 0.262 (0.164–0.364) | 0.0000 | 0.347 | 0.147 (0.092–0.204) |

**Verdict:** the gate's precision (0.618) is far above random (0.262, 95% range 0.164–0.364); none of the 10,000 random draws matched it. Per category: factual: p = 0.000 (clearly better than random); math: p = 0.042 (better than random at the 5% level); reasoning: p = 0.064 (NOT clearly better than random). Overall strength comes mostly from factual; math has only 6 wrong answers.

## Part 2a — Gate precision and recall, 95% bootstrap CI

2,000 resamples of questions (within each category), escalated set kept fixed.

| Metric | Value | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| Precision | 0.618 | 0.491–0.736 |
| Recall | 0.347 | 0.254–0.443 |

**Verdict:** even the low end of the precision interval (0.491) is well above the random level (~0.262).

## Part 2c — CV selector vs TF-IDF (same folds as selector_cv.py)

Each held-out fold is resampled on its own and fold AUROCs are averaged, the same way the reported numbers are computed.

| | Selector | TF-IDF |
|---|---:|---:|
| factual | 0.909 | 0.909 |
| math | 0.836 | 0.740 |
| reasoning | 0.667 | 0.629 |
| **macro** | **0.804** (0.735–0.861) | **0.759** (0.681–0.831) |

- Difference: **+0.045**, 95% CI -0.024 to +0.105
- Share of resamples where the selector is not better (difference ≤ 0): 0.085
- Valid resamples: 1995 of 2000 (a resample is skipped if a held-out fold ends up with no wrong answers)
- Fold winners: factual: tfidf, tfidf, tfidf, tfidf, tfidf; math: unigram, unigram, unigram, unigram, bigram; reasoning: bigram, trigram, bigram, bigram, bigram

**Verdict:** the +0.045 gain is NOT reliably above zero — the interval includes 0. Almost all of the gain comes from math, where only 6 answers are wrong, so treat it as promising but not proven.
